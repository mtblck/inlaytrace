Clear space and size

Keep clear space of at least 25 % of the mark's height on every side. Below each logo's minimum width, step down to the mark; below 32 px, use the favicon.

Colour

Contrast is against white and against Night. 4.5:1 and above is fine for text; 3:1 for large text and graphics.

Typography

Archivo for everything: the wordmark and headings at 700, body copy at 400. It's a free Google Font.

Body copy is set in Archivo at 16 to 18 px with a line height around 1.55. Keep lines to 60 to 75 characters and let the display face carry the personality in headings, so the page stays calm to read.

Don't

  • Stretch, squash, rotate or re-space the logo, or rebuild the wordmark in a live font.
  • Recolour it outside the palette, add shadows, outlines, gradients or effects.
  • Put the light logo on a busy photo or a mid-tone; switch to the dark or one-colour version.
  • Crowd it inside the clear space, or use it smaller than its minimum width.
