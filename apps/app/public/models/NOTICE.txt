Model weights served with the Inlay app, used only in your browser.

u2netp.onnx
  U^2-Net (small), salient object detection, used for background removal.
  Xuebin Qin et al., "U2-Net: Going Deeper with Nested U-Structure for
  Salient Object Detection", Pattern Recognition, 2020.
  Weights: https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net (Apache License 2.0), as
  exported to ONNX by rembg (https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg, MIT).

realesr-general-x4v3.onnx
  Real-ESRGAN, general x4 v3 (SRVGGNetCompact), used for upscaling.
  Xintao Wang et al., "Real-ESRGAN: Training Real-World Blind
  Super-Resolution with Pure Synthetic Data", ICCVW 2021.
  Weights: https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN (BSD 3-Clause License),
  release v0.2.5.0, converted from PyTorch to ONNX (opset 17) unchanged.
