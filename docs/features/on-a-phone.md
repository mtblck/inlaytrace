# On a phone

Below 1024 px wide, the app fits the screen. The header stays at the top,
the image sits under it, the panel scrolls beneath the image, and a dock runs
along the foot of the screen. Once an image is open, *Download SVG* sits at
the top of the panel, so it stays in reach however far you scroll.

## The dock

The yellow button in the middle of the dock is the mode. Tap it to choose
*Clean up*, *Trace* or *Edit* (Clean up only shows for a traced image, not an
opened SVG). The two buttons on each side of it follow the mode:

| Mode | Left | Right |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trace | *Image* (the file, Replace, Start over, Prepare), *Type* (the presets) | *Tune* (Adjust, Target size), *Export* (every format) |
| Edit | *Select* (and Mirror), *Nodes* | *Combine* (jumps to the Combine buttons for the selected shapes), *Draw* (Pen, Shape, Fill, Eraser, Knife) |
| Clean up | *Crop* (and Straighten), *Brush* (and Fill, Heal, Despeckle) | *Erase* (Eraser, Magic wand, Keep, Remove), *Levels* (jumps to Levels and Threshold) |

A button with a small arrow holds more than one tool. Tap it to pick its
tool, then tap it again to list the others. The button then takes the name of
the tool you picked. In Trace, each button shows its part of the panel and
puts the others away.

## The header

In Edit and Clean up, *Undo* and *Redo* are in the header (on a wide screen
they're at the foot of the tool rail). The heart links to Ko-fi, and the ⋯
menu has the theme, the docs, privacy and the source.

## The site

The site's header stays at the top as you scroll, with the docs and the tip
button. On the home page, *Trace an image, free* floats at the foot of the
screen once the hero's own button has scrolled away, and steps aside for the
footer. The three close-ups under *Shapes a designer would draw* become a row
to swipe, with *Source*, *Inlay* and *VTracer* buttons to jump between them.
