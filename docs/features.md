## A typical session

1. Drop, paste or choose an image (or *Try a sample*). It traces straight away
   with the Logo preset.
2. Pick the preset that matches the image and adjust colours, specks,
   smoothing and corners. Check the result in Split and Difference.
3. Open the Edit tab. The view switches to the SVG on its own. Remove a
   background colour, recolour, fix a node or two, or mirror a symmetrical logo.
4. Download the SVG, or the layers zip for printing or cutting.
