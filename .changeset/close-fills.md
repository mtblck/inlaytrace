Logo and Illustration keep a flat colour that sits close to another one, when there's room under the colour cap: a truck logo's neutral-grey grille bars and tyre treads, 0.057 from its blue-grey cab in OKLab, used to trace as the cab, smeared with navy. Only flat art gets this (four in five pixels flat), and gradients' in-between shades are left out, so photos and soft shading trace as before.
