A barely-there gradient in a background (a near-white vignette) no longer traces as ragged bands of close tints: Logo and Illustration join them into one shape, flat in Logo's commonest colour, and one gradient in Illustration. Only close colours the image passes between without an edge are joined, so a pale fill on white stays a shape. On a dump-truck logo the Logo SVG went from 107 to 63 KB.
